The work

What a risk control consultant actually does

Survey the plant

Boilers and pressure vessels, switchgear and transformers, motors, chillers and production machinery. Condition, maintenance history, and whether the practices around the machine match what the machine needs.

Quantify the exposure

What breaks, what it costs to replace, how long the business stops while it is replaced, and which single failure takes the most out of the operation. The loss that matters is rarely the most expensive part.

Write it so it gets done

Recommendations an engineer and an accountant can both act on, and the follow through that turns a report into a changed work practice rather than a filed document.

Twenty years on the machinery side

From a submarine engine room to equipment breakdown,risk control

Lawrence Steven Cosentino has been a risk control consultant at Travelers since 2022, working in equipment breakdown. Before that he was a loss control specialist at Hartford Steam Boiler, where he was recognised in 2019 for exemplary integrity, a senior technician on the emergency response team at Global Foundries, and a maintenance supervisor at Gourmet Boutique. He served eight years in the United States Navy as a nuclear electrician, aboard the submarines USS Jacksonville and USS Missouri.

  • BS in Computer Information Systems, 2005, from the institution now called Manhattan University.
  • Lean Six Sigma Green Belt and Black Belt, Villanova University.
  • Reading for an MBA with a concentration in business analytics.
  • VFW Post 2285, American Legion Post 979, Eastchester Veterans Foundation.
